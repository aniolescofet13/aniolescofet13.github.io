A comprehensive laboratory course at Politecnico di Milano covering the full analytical stack of vehicle dynamics, from tyre contact mechanics through chassis control design and high-fidelity multibody simulation. Working with a SportCar segment model (RWD, 1360 kg, 245/40 R18), the six-chapter report addresses Pacejka Magic Formula tyre modelling including combined slip degradation, vertical load sensitivity, and transient relaxation behavior; vehicle longitudinal dynamics covering mechanical brake distributor sizing, traction and braking manoeuvres, cruise control design, and ABS controller validation against ISO 21994 performance indicators on dry and snow surfaces; vertical dynamics comparing passive, semi-active, and active sky-hook suspension against ISO 8608 road profiles with ISO 2631 comfort assessment; lateral dynamics spanning steady-state cornering (ISO 4138), step steer (ISO 19364), sine-with-dwell stability assessment (ISO 19365), effective axle characteristics, path-follower control with double lane change (ISO 3888), and Torque Vectoring Control; ADAMS Car multibody analysis for suspension geometry sensitivity and full-vehicle response; and a VI-CarRealTime 14-DOF simulation for directional response and lateral stability validation.
