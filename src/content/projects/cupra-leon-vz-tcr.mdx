The CUPRA Leon VZ TCR is CUPRA's flagship touring car, launched in May 2024 and crowned TCR Model of the Year after over 100 victories and 17 titles worldwide in 2025 alone. It competes in TCR Europe, TCR Mexico, TCR China, and the IMSA Michelin Pilot Challenge in the US. With 325 PS and 420 Nm, a redesigned front axle, SADEV gearbox upgrade kit and performance suspension, it is the most powerful front-wheel-drive Leon ever built. A street-legal version limited to 499 units was announced for 2026.

I worked on the software and electronic setup of these race cars, handling vehicle configuration, sensor calibration, CAN communication protocols and making sure the full electronics package was race-ready for track sessions and competition weekends. This included testing at Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya in Montmeló and shakedowns at IDIADA proving grounds.
