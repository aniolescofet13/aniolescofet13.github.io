A motorsport prototype project aimed at combining two electrically distinct vehicle platforms into a single all-wheel-drive architecture. The core challenge was bridging two separate powertrains, each with its own control logic, communication stack and operating envelope, and making them behave as one coherent system under real driving conditions.

I designed and implemented the full control software that tied both platforms together. This covered the inter-system communication layer, the torque distribution strategy between axles, and the validation of stability and drivability across a range of operating conditions from low-speed maneuvering to dynamic motorsport scenarios.
